# Soundtracks for the essays

Curated 10 November 2026. Each essay has one optional official Spotify player after its introductory paragraph, with a direct track link and an expandable listening note. The water essay uses the recording selected by the author.

The pairings follow each essay's emotional movement and argument. Listening notes are editorial interpretations. Exact track identity, credited artists, album edition, duration and current catalog playability were checked through Spotify. Artist, label and estate sources provide recording context. The chosen Robert Wyatt edition is the 1998 remaster of the original studio performance. The full-length Harold Melvin album recording is used. Spotify marks the Paris Paloma and Kendrick Lamar recordings explicit.

## To the Girl Who Was Told to Stop Dreaming

**Lalay Lalay — Kefaya, Elaha Soroor**

Songs Of Our Mothers. 4:26. [Spotify recording](https://open.spotify.com/track/7HLkFnOYchVoIdMaU6pGk4).

A lullaby directed toward a daughter’s future. Its tenderness belongs beside this letter’s hope that a girl can grow, learn, and enter the world with an ordinary freedom.

Recording context. [Source 1](https://kefayaelahasoroor.bandcamp.com/track/lalay-lalay), [Source 2](https://www.etilaatroz.com/87264/songs-of-our-mothers-a-great-album-by-elahe-soroor/).
